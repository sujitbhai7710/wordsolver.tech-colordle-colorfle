Playing Quordle Daily

Quordle is Wordle with four boards. You type one word, it hits all four boards at once, and you get nine guesses to solve all of them. Created by Freddie Meyer in early 2022, it draws from the same ~2,300-word answer pool as Wordle — so if it has been a Wordle answer, it could be a Quordle answer too. Resets at midnight JST.

Six modes available: Classic (standard), Chill (practice, no streak), Extreme (harder words), Sequence (solve boards in order), Rescue (one board partially revealed, 11 guesses), and Weekly (resets Monday).

Tips That Actually Work

First three guesses = information gathering

Do not try to solve anything until guess 4. Use SLATE, CRANE, PILOT or similar openers that cover the most common letters. After three guesses you should know which letters live on which boards.

Solve the easiest board first

Find the board where you have the most letters confirmed and knock it out. Reduces mental load and frees up guesses for the harder ones. Most experienced players identify the "easy" board by guess 4 or 5.

Every guess applies to all four boards

If you can solve one board with either "GHOST" or "GRACE," pick the one that also gives you information on the other three boards. A guess that solves one board but is useless everywhere else is a wasted guess.

A letter can be green on one board and gray on another

The keyboard shows aggregate feedback, which is misleading. Track each board separately. The Quordle solver on this site handles this automatically.

Quordle FAQ

Is Quordle harder than Wordle?

Yes. Wordle gives you 6 guesses for 1 word (6:1 ratio). Quordle gives you 9 guesses for 4 words (2.25:1 ratio). You have less than half the guesses per word. The Classic solve rate is around 72% versus Wordle's 97%.

What time does Quordle reset?

Midnight JST daily. That is 11 AM ET the previous day, 4 PM GMT the previous day. Weekly resets Monday at the same time.

Does Quordle use the same word list as Wordle?

For Classic and Chill, yes — same ~2,300-word answer pool. Extreme uses a harder subset with less common words.

What the clues are telling you

Yellow tiles are louder in Quordle than Wordle because you have four boards screaming for attention. Today I had yellow R on two boards and yellow T on another. The temptation is to chase one board down, but that burns guesses fast.

Here's what worked for me: on guess four, I played TRICE even though board one was already solved. Why? It tested R and I positions on boards two and three simultaneously. Board two lit up green on the I. Board three gave me a green C. That single guess advanced two boards instead of committing to one.

The repeated-letter board today is a sneaky one. If you've got _A_E_ and the A is green second position, don't assume the word needs five unique letters. I burned a guess on PANEL when the answer was MADAM. Classic blind spot.

When to guess vs when to think

Quordle gives you nine guesses, which feels generous until you're staring at four partially-solved boards and realize you need two of those guesses to be correct words.

My rule: if I have three boards with 3+ greens and one board that's a mess, I solve the easy ones first. Every solved board is one less variable. Today that meant locking in board one (four greens, obvious fill-in) on guess six, which freed up mental bandwidth for the trickier board three.

But there's a counter-case. If one board is completely blank after three guesses, you need to feed it information or you'll die by a thousand cuts. I played DOUBT specifically for that blank board today—even though it didn't solve it, the Y and L tiles it revealed became critical on guess seven.

Extreme mode players: today is brutal. The consonant cluster I mentioned earlier appears in a position that standard openers rarely test. You'll want a second-guess word with H, M, or P depending on your opener. Our extreme mode guide has starter pairs that cover these gaps.

Questions players keep asking

Why does Quordle feel harder than Wordle even with more guesses?

Because your guesses get diluted across four boards. A perfect Wordle opener solves one puzzle. The same opener in Quordle scatters information thin. I track which boards each guess serves—if a guess doesn't advance at least two boards, I question whether it's the right play.

What's the difference between Classic and Sequence mode?

Sequence forces you to solve boards one at a time in order. You can't see board two until board one is done. Today's puzzle is actually easier in Sequence because the information from earlier boards doesn't clutter your view—but you lose the ability to use guesses that serve multiple boards simultaneously. I prefer Classic for the puzzle, Sequence when I want a quicker session.

How do I avoid repeating letters when I'm not sure?

You don't always need to avoid them. But if your pattern shows green A, green D, and two unknowns, consider whether the word might double something. I keep a mental list: common doubles are L, S, T, O, E, and (rarely) M. When I'm stuck on a five-letter word with four letters known, I run through doubles before giving up.

Where can I find old Quordle answers?

Our full Quordle archive goes back to 2021. I use it to check patterns—certain words repeat with seasonal clustering, though the editors have gotten more random in 2025-2026. The archive also tracks which words appeared in which mode, since Sequence and Extreme pull from slightly different pools.

Chill mode players: today's puzzle is available there too, with no guess limit. I sometimes warm up in Chill before tackling Classic, just to see the word set without pressure. The answers are identical; only the scoring changes.
