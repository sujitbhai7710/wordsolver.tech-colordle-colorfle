How today's clues work

LoLdle gives you one champion to guess and six tries to get there. The clues cover gender, position, species, region, and release year — and that last one catches people out more than you'd think. If you've been playing League since 2013, you probably know the old champions cold. But if a 2024 release comes up and you haven't kept up, you're going to have a bad time.

Classic mode splits attributes into match, partial, and miss. A green box means exact match — your guess and the answer share that attribute. Yellow means close but not quite (like both being mages but one is burst and one is control). Gray means it's not a match at all. Four modes total: Classic, Quote, Ability, and Splash Art. Each one tests a different part of your champion knowledge.

Two regional variants — America and Europe — run separate answer pools. So the Classic answer for NA might be completely different from EU on the same day. This page tracks both.

The mode that trips people up

Splash Art mode is where most players lose their streak. The game crops a tiny section of a splash art — could be a weapon edge, a piece of armor, or just a color gradient in the background — and you have to figure out which champion it belongs to. If you don't actively browse splash art galleries, you're guessing blind.

Quote mode is the second-hardest. Everyone knows Yasuo's "Hasagi!" but when you get some obscure line from a champion who got reworked three years ago, you're in trouble. The wiki has every voice line if you want to study up, but honestly, just playing more ARAM helps — you see champions you'd never pick in draft.

Ability mode sounds hard but isn't as bad as you'd think. Most ability icons have a distinctive color palette tied to the champion's theme. Once you've seen a few hundred, you start recognizing them by style alone.

Quick tips from the community

Start with a weird champion in Classic. Someone with uncommon attributes — like a manaless yordle or a non-human support — splits the pool fast. Gnar, Kennen, or Rek'Sai are solid openers because their attribute combos are rare.

Learn which champions are resourceless. Mana is the default, but energy (Zed, Akali, Lee Sin), fury (Renekton, Tryndamere), and no-cost (Riven, Katarina, Yasuo) champions are a minority. If the answer doesn't use mana, you've just eliminated 70% of the roster.

Use the solver for Splash mode. There's no shame in it. Enter the colors and shapes you see, and the solver narrows it down. Better to use help than to stare at a crop of a cape for ten minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time does LoLdle reset?

Midnight UTC. If you're on EST, that's 7 PM the night before.

Why do America and Europe have different answers?

Separate answer pools for each region. Keeps things fresh and prevents spoilers across time zones.

How many champions are in the pool?

Over 160, and it grows every time Riot releases a new one. New champions are tricky because fewer people have memorized their attributes yet.

Is there a LoLdle app?

No app — it's browser-based. Works fine on mobile though.

Today's puzzle in context

Quinn sits in a weird spot in the champion pool. She is technically a marksman, but her playstyle in the actual game often leans into skirmisher territory, especially with how she roams. In the context of LoLdle, she is a classic "region trap." Players often associate her with Noxus because of her lore conflicts or assume she is from the Freljord due to the eagle companion. Today, the region hint was the primary filter. Once you locked in Demacia, the pool shrinks dramatically. You are left with Garen, Lux, Jarvan IV, and a few others. The gender hint cuts that in half immediately. The real tell is the attack type. Physical damage narrows it to the AD carries and fighters. If you guessed Lux first, you wasted a life. If you guessed Garen, you misread the range. Quinn's specific niche as a ranged physical damage dealer from Demacia makes her the only logical fit once those three attributes align.

Her release date back in 2013 puts her in the middle era of champion design, where kits were getting more mobile but hadn't reached the current complexity ceiling. Knowing release windows helps, but today the region and class were the heavy lifters. Which attributes matter most today

Region and Class were the heavy hitters. Gender was a close third, but it acted more as a confirmer than a driver. Here is the breakdown of why these specific attributes carried the weight:

  • Region (Demacia): This is the biggest filter. Demacia has a distinct visual language and lore identity. Champions here are often human, use steel or light magic, and value honor. Confusing Demacia with Piltover or Ionia is a common mistake, but the aesthetic is distinct.
  • Class (Marksman): This separates the ADCs from the mages and tanks. In LoLdle, "Marksman" strictly means ranged, auto-attack focused champions. This excludes fighters like Garen or mages like Lux, even if they deal damage.
  • Gender (Female): While obvious in hindsight, this eliminates half the Demacian roster. Without this, you are guessing between Quinn, Garen, Jarvan, and Xin Zhao (if you count him). With it, you are down to Quinn and Lux.

Species was the curveball. Quinn is human, but Valor is a bird. Some players overthink this and guess a Yordle or a vastayan. Stick to the champion's base species, not their companion. If you need to double-check a champion's specific classification or region, the LoLdle solver tool updates its database with every new release and rework, ensuring your filters are accurate. Don't rely on memory alone; the roster changes too often with reworks like the recent updates to older champions.

Questions players keep asking

Why is Quinn classified as a Marksman if she melee dives?

Quinn's kit allows her to dive with her ultimate and execute with Skystrike, but her base stats and itemization path are strictly Marksman. She scales with attack speed and critical strike chance, not ability power or tank stats. In LoLdle, classification is based on the primary role and scaling, not momentary playstyle quirks. If they build crit and attack speed, they are a Marksman.

Does Valor count as a separate species in LoLdle?

No. The species attribute refers to the champion themselves. Quinn is human. Valor is an eagle, but he is part of her kit, not a separate entity for classification purposes. If the answer was a Yordle or a vastayan, the species hint would reflect that. Do not let companions confuse your species filter.

How do I remember which champions are from Demacia?

Demacians typically have names that sound European or classical, wear metal armor, and use swords or spears. They rarely use heavy magic (except Lux) and focus on physical prowess or light-based abilities. A good trick is to group them by their lore connections: Garen, Lux, Jarvan IV, Xin Zhao, and Quinn form the core. If you are unsure, check the regional archive to see the full list and their visual cues.

What if the region hint is wrong?

Riot occasionally updates lore, which can shift a champion's region. However, LoLdle usually sticks to the current canonical region. If a hint feels off, check the date of the last lore update. For today's puzzle, Demacia was correct. If you ever suspect a lore shift has occurred, the solver notes these changes in the changelog, so you aren't working with outdated data.

Tomorrow's puzzle will likely reset the difficulty curve. Keep your region filters ready and don't sleep on the less popular champions. They show up more often than you think when the common picks are exhausted. Good luck, and may your next guess be the correct one.
